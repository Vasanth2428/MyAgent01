  Shape [1]: Picture 4 | Type: PICTURE (13)

  Shape [2]: Content Placeholder 3 | Type: PLACEHOLDER (14)
    Text Frame Paragraphs count: 2
      Para [0]: HeLa cervical cancer cells were cultured under standard conditions and seeded into 96-well plates. The cells were treated with different concentrations of Tinospora cordifolia leaf extract and incubated for 48 hours. Cytotoxicity was assessed using the MTT assay, which measures mitochondrial metabolic activity as an indicator of cell viability. After incubation with MTT reagent, the formed formazan crystals were dissolved and absorbance was measured spectrophotometrically. The percentage of viable cells was calculated relative to untreated controls, and the IC₅₀ value was determined to quantify the anticancer effect.
